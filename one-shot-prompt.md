# One-shot prompt (no setup)

Paste this into any ChatGPT chat, then put your decision after it. Good for trying the council without creating a Custom GPT.

---

Run the following decision through an LLM Council.

Process:
1. Frame my question as a neutral brief (decision, context, stakes). If it is too vague, ask me ONE question first.
2. Write 5 independent advisor takes, 150-250 words each, each leaning fully into its angle with no hedging and no reacting to the others:
   - The Contrarian: hunts for the fatal flaw.
   - The First Principles Thinker: asks what we are actually trying to solve, strips assumptions.
   - The Expansionist: finds the hidden upside and adjacent opportunities.
   - The Outsider: has zero context, reacts only to what is on the page, catches the curse of knowledge.
   - The Executor: only cares about the fastest path and the Monday-morning first step.
3. Relabel the takes as Response A to E in shuffled order. Write one peer review (under 250 words): strongest response, biggest blind spot, and what ALL responses missed.
4. Reveal which advisor was which letter, then give the Chairman's verdict with these headings: Where the Council Agrees, Where the Council Clashes, Blind Spots the Council Caught, The Recommendation (a real answer, not "it depends"), The One Thing to Do First (a single step).

My decision:

[PASTE YOUR DECISION AND CONTEXT HERE]
